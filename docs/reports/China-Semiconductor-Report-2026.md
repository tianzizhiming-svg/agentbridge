# China Semiconductor Report - 2026 Edition

## Foreword

The semiconductor industry has arrived at a trillion-dollar inflection point. Global semiconductor market size is projected to reach approximately $1 trillion in 2026 — the strongest upcycle in a decade-8. In China, the integrated circuit industry's sales revenue (design, manufacturing, packaging, and testing) exceeded RMB 1.7 trillion in 2025, with growth exceeding 19% year-over-year-8. When including equipment, components, and materials, the total surpassed RMB 1.9 trillion-8.
But the headline numbers tell only part of the story. China's semiconductor industry is no longer defined by the "chokepoint" anxiety of past years. It is defined by a fundamental transformation — from point breakthroughs to full-chain evolution, from import dependency to structural self-sufficiency, from "catching up" to "running alongside"-1. This is a sector that produced 334.2 billion integrated circuits in the first seven months of 2026, up 23.1% year-over-year-2. This is a sector where integrated circuit manufacturing value-added output surged 67.3% year-over-year in H1 2026-2.
This is not a superficial summary. This is the strategic analysis that covers: the true scale of China's semiconductor self-sufficiency drive, the trillion-dollar market inflection point and what it means for global competition, the state of play across each value chain segment — design, manufacturing, packaging, equipment, and materials, the key companies that are reshaping the competitive landscape, the structural constraints that still limit China's ambitions, the geopolitical risks that continue to shape the industry, and the outlook for H2 2026 and beyond.

## Part II: The Inflection Point — Why 2026 Is Different

### 2.1 The Trillion-Dollar Milestone

2026 is the year the global semiconductor market is projected to cross the $1 trillion threshold — "the strongest upcycle in a decade"-8. This is not a cyclical recovery. It is a structural shift driven by AI.
The AI factor:
Global AI computing power is projected to account for over 80% of all computing power by 2026-5
AI accelerator demand from 2022 to 2026 increased 11x (TSMC data)-5
The "training-to-inference" paradigm shift has opened a commercialization window for AI chips-10

### 2.2 China's Position in the Upcycle

Chinese semiconductor companies are riding this wave. Key indicators:
Integrated circuit manufacturing value-added growth: +67.3% (H1 2026)-2
Investment in integrated circuit manufacturing: +11.5% (first 7 months)-2
Investment in electronic circuit manufacturing: +57.7% (first 7 months)-2

### 2.3 The "AI+Semiconductor" Convergence

The semiconductor industry is being structurally reshaped by AI integration. The following sectors are the primary growth engines-8-10:

| Application | Growth Driver |
|---|---|
| AI Servers | Training and inference compute |
| Physical AI | Robotics and embodied intelligence |
| Automotive Electronics | Intelligent and electric vehicles |
| Data Centers | Expansion of AI infrastructure |

## Part III: Value Chain Analysis — Where China Stands

### 3.1 Chip Design (IC Design)

2025 revenue: RMB 835.7 billion (+29.4% YoY)-1
Key developments:
China now accounts for over one-quarter of the global $430 billion design market-1
Design enterprises have exceeded 3,900-10
Enterprises with sales exceeding RMB 100 million have surpassed 800-1
The industry structure is transitioning from "small and scattered" to "strong and excellent"-1
Competitive position:
Leading domestic design companies include HiSilicon (Shenzhen), UNISOC, GigaDevice, and OmniVision (now Chinese-owned)-3-11
EDA tool localization has moved from policy-driven to application-penetration, with mature-node tools now providing a solid foundation-1

### 3.2 Wafer Manufacturing (晶圆制造)

2025 revenue: Global foundry revenue reached RMB 1.1485 trillion (+25.46% YoY); China's foundry revenue reached $16.4 billion-7-10
Top 10 foundries captured 96.27% of global market share-10.
Key players:

| Company | Position | Key Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| SMIC (中芯国际) | China's largest; 6th globally | High CAPEX (>90% of revenue); government support; dependency on foreign equipment for sub-10nm-11 |
| Hua Hong (华虹) | China's second-largest | Mature-node focus (90nm+); 40% state-owned; reported 7nm capability-11 |
| CXMT (长鑫存储) | Leading DRAM manufacturer | ~$8.6B IPO in July 2026; DDR5 production; HBM3 planned; Q1 revenue surged 7x YoY-2-11 |
| YMTC (长江存储) | Leading NAND manufacturer | NAND focus; expanding into DRAM; cooperating with CXMT on HBMs-11 |

The 2026 expansion wave: Major manufacturers announced significant capacity expansions:
Changdian Technology (长电科技): RMB 7.8 billion advanced packaging plant in Shanghai's "Oriental Chip Valley"-5
Xinlian Integration (芯联集成): RMB 20 billion 12-inch automotive-grade chip line-5
Total disclosed expansions by A-listed semiconductor companies: Nearly RMB 45 billion in 2026-5

### 3.3 Packaging and Testing (封测)

Market size: China's packaging and testing market is projected to exceed RMB 420 billion by 2030-1
Advanced packaging shift:
2025 advanced packaging share: 39%
2030 projected advanced packaging share: 48%+
2.5D/3D packaging capacity remains a critical bottleneck until at least 2027-5
Key players: Jiangsu Changdian Technology (长电科技), Tongfu Microelectronics (通富微电), Tianshui Huatian (天水华天)-5

### 3.4 Semiconductor Equipment (半导体设备)

Market scale: China remains the world's largest semiconductor equipment market at $55.9 billion (2025), maintaining this position for six consecutive years-1.
Domestic equipment penetration:
35% equipment self-sufficiency rate in 2025-1
In new capacity additions, domestic equipment accounted for 55% of total value in 2025 — ahead of the national 50% target-10
Etching and cleaning equipment self-sufficiency has exceeded 60%-10
Key domestic equipment manufacturers:

| Company | Focus Area | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|---|
| Naura (北方华创) | Etching, thin-film deposition | China's largest; among global top 5 by revenue; 14nm+ mature-node focus-11 |
| AMEC (中微公司) | Etching, deposition | Reported penetration into 5nm process nodes; serves TSMC, Samsung-11 |
| Hwatsing (华海清科) | CMP equipment | New entrant in equipment space-3 |

The equipment constraint: Despite progress, China's equipment manufacturers still depend on foreign suppliers for precision components-11. Access to EUV lithography equipment remains blocked for sub-10nm manufacturing due to US export restrictions-11.

### 3.5 Semiconductor Materials (半导体材料)

The materials sector is moving from low-end trials to "deep-water" breakthroughs, targeting high-barrier areas like EUV photoresist and polishing fluids-1.
Key domestic material players:
Hubei Xingfu Electronic Materials
Jiangsu Huahai Chengke New Materials
Zhongjuxin Technology
Ningbo Jiangfeng Electronic Materials-3

## Part V: The Self-Sufficiency Drive — Progress and Constraints

### 5.1 The Government's Target

The self-sufficiency drive has multiple dimensions:

| Area | Progress |
|---|---|
| Foundry capacity relative to local electronics manufacturing needs | 71%-7 |
| Foundry capacity relative to domestic demand | 112%-7 |
| Domestic equipment in new capacity | 55% (exceeded 50% target)-10 |
| Equipment self-sufficiency (overall) | 35%-1 |
| Equipment self-sufficiency potential by 2030 | 52%-7 |

### 5.2 The Structural Constraints

| Constraint | Impact |
|---|---|
| US export controls | Block access to equipment for sub-10nm manufacturing-11 |
| EUV lithography | ASML equipment remains inaccessible |
| Precision components | Equipment manufacturers depend on foreign suppliers-11 |
| Advanced process gap | China's leading foundry still lag behind TSMC/Samsung in advanced nodes |
| High R&D costs | Low profitability despite government support; SMIC's CAPEX often exceeds 90% of revenue-11 |

### 5.3 The Balancing Act

Pierre Cambou, principal analyst at Yole Group, described China's challenge: "The Chinese government is pushing industry-wide to build a self-sustaining semiconductor ecosystem, while at the same time maintaining an 'arm's length' competitive stance from the U.S. in the emerging AI technology arena. Ultimately, balancing breadth and focus is the impossible task being asked today of the semiconductor industry in China."-7
China is building a broad, vertically integrated ecosystem while navigating export controls and global competition across advanced technologies, including AI-7.

## Part VI: The AI-Driven Demand Surge

### 6.1 The Data Story

Compute demand growth:
Global computing power: 2.1 ZFLOPS (2025), projected to 16 ZFLOPS+ by 2030-5
AI computing power share: Projected to exceed 80% in 2026-5
Memory demand:
AI workloads increasingly require High Bandwidth Memory (HBM)
China currently depends on foreign HBMs for AI processors-11
CXMT plans older-generation HBM3 mass production; YMTC collaborating on HBMs-11

### 6.2 Export-Driven Expansion

China's electronics manufacturing base remains export-driven, with electronic manufacturing representing 158% of domestic electronic demand-7. This export orientation creates both opportunity (scale) and vulnerability (trade policy).

## Part VII: Outlook and Key Signals

### 7.1 What to Watch

**$1 trillion milestone tracking:** The projected crossing of the global $1 trillion market threshold will be a psychological and strategic turning point-8
Domestic equipment penetration acceleration: The 55% figure in new capacity is promising — can it reach the 2030 potential of 52% overall?-7-10
Memory output expansion: CXMT's HBM3 production timeline and YMTC's DRAM expansion will determine China's ability to close the memory gap-11
Advanced packaging capacity: 2.5D/3D packaging remains a bottleneck. Resolution will shift the competitive landscape-5
SMIC/Hua Hong financials: These companies' profitability trends will indicate whether scale economies are being achieved-11

### 7.2 Risk Factors

Geopolitical risk: US export controls could tighten further, restricting access to critical equipment-11
Profitability risk: High R&D and CAPEX spending may continue to suppress margins-11
Technology gap risk: Progress in advanced nodes may slow if equipment access is constrained
Overcapacity risk: The 2026 expansion wave could lead to structural oversupply in certain segments-5
Supply chain risk: Dependency on foreign precision components remains a vulnerability-11
