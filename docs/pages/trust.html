About AgentBridge Atlas

Who we are, how buying works, and the plain terms that apply to every purchase.

Who we are

🌉

AgentBridge is an independent research and data shop focused on China's digital ecosystem — university quality reports, employment data, policy research, travel knowledge, and human guides for navigating life in China.

Our reports are written by a single analyst operating as 阿扉 (afield). Every report is built strictly on the source institution's published data; where information is missing, the report says so and points to how it can be verified, rather than guessing.

The store is delivered as a static, machine-readable catalog with a live backend API. Developer products settle via the x402 protocol in USDC on Base; human services can be billed by Stripe, PayPal, or crypto.

What you're buying

  • Developer Store (shelf A) — pay-per-call APIs for AI agents. You trigger a 402 challenge, settle with any Base wallet, and receive the content automatically. No accounts, no API keys.
  • Human Services (shelf B) — guides and reports for people. Billed by Stripe / PayPal / crypto, delivered by email, typically within 1–2 business days.
  • Free samples — select full-length reports are free to download, no purchase required, so you can judge our quality before paying.

Pricing & payment

Prices are listed per item. Most reports are priced per copy in USDC on Base (chain eip155:8453). A few low-priced items — web fetches and travel handbooks — are priced per unit as marked.

Payment methods differ by shelf:

  • x402 (developer): USDC on Base, settled automatically by your wallet or agent.
  • Stripe / PayPal / crypto (human): for services and reports purchased by people.

Delivery

  • Developer items: delivered immediately by the API after settlement.
  • Human-service items: delivered to the email you provide at checkout, within 1–2 business days.
  • Free samples: instant download from this site.

Refunds & fair use

No refunds on delivered digital content. Because our reports and data are delivered instantly and are not returnable, all sales of delivered digital content are final.
  • If your purchase cannot be delivered (e.g. an item we can't fulfill), you get a full refund — no questions asked.
  • Report inaccuracies are taken seriously. If a report misstates a fact from the source material, email us and we'll correct it or refund at our discretion.
  • Purchased reports are for your own use. Redistribution of full paid content is not permitted.
