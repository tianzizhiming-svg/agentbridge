# AgentBridge Atlas

## The AI-native marketplace for machine commerce


AgentBridge Atlas is the marketplace layer built on top of AgentBridge.

The internet was designed for humans.
APIs were designed for software.

But AI agents need something new:

A way to discover, evaluate, purchase, and consume digital capabilities autonomously.

Atlas is an experiment toward that future.

---

# What is AgentBridge Atlas?

AgentBridge Atlas is a machine-readable catalog and commerce layer for AI agents.

It connects:

- AI agents
- digital assets
- APIs
- data providers
- human-created knowledge
- autonomous payments

through standardized discovery and x402 payment infrastructure.
