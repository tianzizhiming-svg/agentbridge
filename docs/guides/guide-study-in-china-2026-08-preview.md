# Part I: Understanding Your Visa Options


## 1.1 X1 Visa — Long-Term Study (More Than 180 Days)

Who needs it: Students enrolled in degree programs (Bachelor's, Master's, PhD), one-year exchange programs, or any study program lasting longer than 180 days .

Key characteristics:

Valid for single entry into China

Allows stay of 30 days after arrival

Must be converted to a Residence Permit within those 30 days

Can be extended while in China (subject to university verification)

The critical 30-day rule: The X1 visa is a transit visa. It does not grant you permission to remain in China for your entire study period. The Residence Permit is what legally allows your long-term stay . Missing the 30-day deadline results in overstay fines and potential visa cancellation.


## 1.2 X2 Visa — Short-Term Study (Up to 180 Days)

Who needs it: Students enrolled in short-term programs — language courses, semester-long exchange programs, summer/winter schools, visiting scholars .

Key characteristics:

Valid for stay up to 180 days

Cannot be converted to a Residence Permit

Cannot be extended beyond the allowed period

Students must leave China and re-enter if they need to stay longer

For visiting scholars and short-term researchers: If you are coming for a few weeks, you may need an F visa (for exchange, visit, study activities) rather than X2 — check with your host institution .


## 1.3 What NOT to Do

Do not enter China on a Tourist Visa (L Visa) with the intention of switching to a student visa.

If you enter on an L Visa, you may be required to leave China and re-enter to obtain the correct visa status — which is expensive, time-consuming, and operationally complicated .


# Part II: Pre-Departure — Documents You Must Obtain


## 2.1 The JW201/JW202/DQ Forms — Your Most Important Documents

These forms are issued by the Chinese Ministry of Education and are required for your X visa application . Without them, you cannot apply for a student visa.


| Form | Who Needs It | Issued By |
| --- | --- | --- |
| JW201 | CSC Scholarship students | CSC application system |
| JW202 | Full-time degree students, one-year exchange programs | University, via Study in China system |
| DQ | Short-term students (one semester, non-degree) | University, via Study in China system |

How to obtain and verify them:

Your university applies for the form on your behalf after your admission is confirmed

You receive an email from the Study in China system (studyinchina.edu.cn) with a download link

You must download and print the form — on a computer, not a smartphone/tablet (the system is not mobile-compatible)

Verify the form on the Study in China website after downloading

IMPORTANT: The system only allows you to download the form a maximum of 10 times. Make sure you save the file securely .

IMPORTANT: Use the same email address you used for your university application. The system will not recognize a different email address .

How to confirm your form type:

JW201: CSC scholarship students

JW202: Full-time Bachelor's, Master's, PhD, and one-year visiting/exchange programs

DQ: Non-degree Chinese language programs and one-semester exchange programs


## 2.2 Admission Notice

You will receive an electronic copy of your Admission Notice with the university seal. You must:

Download it from your university's application system

Print it in color

Use the printed color copy for your visa application

Note: Only materials provided through the university's official application system and official contact email are considered genuine and valid .


## 2.3 Passport Requirements


| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum validity | 6 months beyond the intended stay |
| Blank visa pages | At least one blank page |
| Photocopy | Copy of the passport information page |


## 2.4 Visa Application Form and Photo

Complete the Visa Application Form of the People's Republic of China online

Most applicants need to make an online appointment via the AVAS (Appointment for Visa Application Submission) website

One recent color passport photo, bareheaded, full-faced, against a light background


## 2.5 Proof of Legal Stay (For Applications Outside Your Home Country)

If you are not applying for your visa in your home country, you need:

Original and copy of your valid documents proving legal stay, residence, work, or study in the current country
